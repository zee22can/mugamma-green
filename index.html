About us

A climate advisory and digital tools firm built for practical impact

Mu Gamma Consultants International Inc. (MGI Canada) helps organizations understand and act on ESG, climate, water and sustainability expectations. We combine scientific and policy expertise with digital tools and training, so that organizations of every size can turn requirements into clear, workable plans.

Our work draws on a global network of experts from international organizations, academic institutions and research bodies, and is delivered with a clear focus on Canadian regulation and Canadian small and mid-sized businesses.

Working with Mu Gamma

We support organizations that want to meet environmental and sustainability expectations without building a large team of their own.

  • Who we serve. Small and mid-sized businesses, industry groups, public bodies and development organizations.
  • What you can expect. Plain-language advice, evidence-based analysis and tools your own people can use.
  • Where our work reaches. From the regulations that apply to your business to water, plastics and climate programs in communities.

Our mission

To help Canadian organizations, especially small and mid-sized businesses, act on ESG, climate and water challenges through practical advisory services, digital tools and capacity building. Our leadership sets clear strategy, manages resources and partnerships responsibly, and communicates openly with clients and stakeholders. We turn complex regulation and scientific evidence into decisions that people can carry out.

Our vision

To be a trusted Canadian advisory partner whose work lets every organization, whatever its size, manage sustainability risk with confidence and contribute to a resilient, low-carbon and water-secure future.

Services

How we help you act on sustainability

Clear advice and practical support, shaped around the size and needs of your organization.

ESG compliance

Clear guidance on ESG expectations for Canadian organizations, from understanding what applies to you to preparing the evidence and reporting stakeholders ask for.

Climate resilience

Advisory support that helps you understand climate risks to water, operations, and communities, and plan responses that hold up over time.

Circular economy

Support for reducing waste and pollution, including plastics, and for redesigning materials and processes so more value stays in use.

Macro and micro plastics pollution

Assessment and action on plastic pollution at every scale, from visible waste in rivers, shorelines and supply chains to microplastics in water, soil and products.

Policy framework and governance

Support to design, review and strengthen environmental policies, regulations and institutional arrangements, so that clear rules and responsibilities lead to real results.

Knowledge and capacity building

Training, courses and learning programs that give professionals and institutions the skills and evidence they need to carry sustainability work forward on their own.
