MU GAMMA CANADA WEBSITE: UPLOAD STEPS

Files in this folder (everything must sit at the TOP level of your GitHub repository):
  index.html        home page
  assessment.html   self-assessment page
  logos/            company and partner logos
  sdg/              the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals icons (used in assessment results)

Steps
1. Unzip this package on your computer.
2. Open your repository on github.com and click Add file, then Upload files.
3. Open the unzipped mugamma-website folder, select ALL items inside it
   (index.html, assessment.html, the logos folder and the sdg folder) and drag them into the GitHub page.
   Do not drag the mugamma-website folder itself.
4. Scroll down and click Commit changes.
5. Wait for the green tick under the Actions tab, then open your site and press Ctrl+F5.

Old file to delete from the repository (no longer used): team.html, index-light.html

Things you can edit later
  - E-learning "Try now" button: in index.html search for "E-LEARNING LINK".
  - Project cards: search for "OUR PROJECTS".
  - LinkedIn buttons: search for linkedin.com and replace with each person's profile address.
  - Job applications: search for "CAREERS=" and put a form service address in "endpoint".
