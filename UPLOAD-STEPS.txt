MU GAMMA CANADA WEBSITE: UPLOAD STEPS

Everything below must sit at the TOP level of your GitHub repository:
  index.html        home page
  assessment.html   self-assessment page
  logos/            partner logos and company logo
  sdg/              the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals icons

1. Unzip this package on your computer.
2. Open your repository on github.com, click Add file, then Upload files.
3. Open the unzipped mugamma-website folder, select ALL items inside it and drag them into the GitHub page.
   Do not drag the mugamma-website folder itself.
4. Click Commit changes.
5. Wait for the green tick under Actions, then open your site and press Ctrl+F5.

Delete from the repository if present: team.html, index-light.html
