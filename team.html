About

Leads Canada operations at MGI Canada. A water and sustainability professional with more than five years of experience conducting water audits that help industries cut water use and costs, across textile, automotive, food and beverage, chemical, steel, coal mining, and power sectors. Holds a Master's in Water Science and Governance from TERI University and post-graduate certificates in Project Management and Global Supply Chain Management from Algonquin College.

Education

  • Master's in Water Science and Governance, TERI University
  • Post-graduate certificates in Project Management and Global Supply Chain Management, Algonquin College

Experience

  • Manager, Canada Operations, Mu Gamma Consultants International (MGI Canada)
  • More than five years conducting water audits for industry

Areas of expertise

  • Industrial water audits and water-use reduction
  • Cost savings through better water management
  • Sectors: textile, automotive, food and beverage, chemical, steel, coal mining, and power
